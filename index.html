The same seed gives the same terrain, but every world is its own separate world, even with the same seed, so nobody can land in yours by accident. To play together, use the Friends list or share your world's join code.
Mode
Brawl: fight waves of mobs in a closed 20x20 arena. Earn points for every kill and spend them in the shop between waves. The other world options are ignored.
Map
Hide & Seek uses Creative rules with hidden nametags. Pick a map: each is a closed 80x80 arena with a boundary wall and a height limit. In multiplayer the host starts a round (button or H): a random player becomes the seeker and is frozen and blind for 30 seconds while everyone else hides. Then the seeker has 5 minutes to tag hiders by clicking or tapping near them, and every tagged hider becomes another seeker.
